How scoring and scheduling work
Only the first answer on a card counts. A replay after a wrong answer is shown as a retry and never raises the score, and each score line carries its 95% interval because a small number of trials cannot pin an accuracy down. Chance is 25% for one of four tones, 25% for one of four syllables, 25% for one of four shapes, and 50% for same-or-different. Guess-corrected scores stay hidden until 60 first attempts on that task.
Four task types are mixed at random: which tone, which syllable (same tone, a near syllable as the distractor), same tone or different (two clips that differ in exactly one thing), and which pitch shape. No task can run more than twice in a row, and the mix keeps a session from becoming one repeated question.
Cards come back on a widening ladder (1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 75, 160, 365 days). A card that is missed drops two boxes and returns sooner; cards that are missed three times inside thirty days are marked quietly as leeches and come back at half the interval. A session is up to 20 cards: warm-ups from the weakest cards, the new cards you asked for, then whatever is most overdue, drawn at random from the eight most urgent. One card per syllable per session, no tone above 40% of the slots, and never more than two cards in a row on the same tone.
Update behavior
When a new deployment is available, the app checks for a newer service worker and shows a reload prompt. That should let you update without manually clearing Safari data most of the time.
